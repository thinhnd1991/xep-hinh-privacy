Privacy Policy – Magic Cube Master

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Camera: the app asks for camera access only when you use the "Scan cube" feature to import a physical puzzle cube. Images are processed on your device to read sticker colours; they are not saved, not uploaded, and never leave your device. You can use every other feature without granting camera access.

The app has no ads, no in-app purchases, and needs no internet connection. Best times, language and sound settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Children: the app does not knowingly collect any information from children or from any other user.
